Content Studio

A personal, self-hosted content calendar used by one creator to plan short videos and schedule them on their own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Runs only on the owner's own computer (local address 127.0.0.1). It is not a public service and has no user sign-up.
  • Keeps a calendar of the owner's videos with titles, descriptions and planned publish times.
  • After the owner connects their YouTube channel with Google sign-in, it uploads the owner's videos to that channel as private with a scheduled publish time, so YouTube releases them at the planned moment.
  • It can change the scheduled time of a video it uploaded, or remove such a video, only when the owner presses the corresponding button.

Google and YouTube data

Content Studio uses the YouTube Data API. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never sent to any other server. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
