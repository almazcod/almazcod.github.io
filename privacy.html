Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Content Studio ("the app") is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. This policy explains what happens with Google and YouTube data when the app is connected to a YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

  • Google sign-in tokens (access and refresh tokens) obtained through Google OAuth after the owner grants permission.
  • YouTube channel name and ID of the connected channel, to show which channel is connected.
  • Videos uploaded by the app: their IDs, status and scheduled publish time, so the app can show progress, reschedule or remove them at the owner's request.

Scopes used: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube.force-ssl (update the publish time or delete a video that the app uploaded, only on the owner's command).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedule their publication. The app does not read, analyze or collect viewers' data, comments, analytics or any other channel content.

Storage and sharing

  • All data is stored locally on the owner's computer in a private file. There is no server, database or cloud storage operated by the app.
  • Data is sent only to Google/YouTube APIs over HTTPS. It is never sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can disconnect YouTube in the app at any time; this deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked in the Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Content Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
